# YouTube Research Archive

## Version: 1.1

## Key Insights
- Channel branding consistency improves subscriber retention.
- Short descriptions (3-4 sentences) with natural language perform better than keyword-stuffed descriptions.
- Thumbnail text should be readable at small sizes.
- Weekly upload frequency maintains audience engagement for children's content.
