# QUALITY WORKFLOW — Quality Assurance Pipeline

## Purpose
Defines the complete quality assurance workflow for generated episodes.

## Quality Checks (Every Episode)

### Story QA
- Confirm one clear value taught.
- Confirm gentle conflict.
- Confirm earned resolution.
- Confirm consistent characters.
- Confirm natural writing.
- Confirm emotional storytelling.

### Writing QA
- Confirm every sentence adds value.
- Confirm conversational English.
- Confirm short spoken sentences.
- Confirm no filler, no repetition.
- Confirm no robotic language.
- Confirm no generic AI phrases.

### Islamic QA
- Confirm accurate references.
- Confirm no invented hadith.
- Confirm no invented Quran citations.
- Confirm no disputed opinions as facts.
- Confirm child-friendly explanations.
- Confirm source references included.

### SEO QA
- Confirm natural descriptions.
- Confirm no keyword stuffing.
- Confirm clean metadata.
- Confirm brand references included.
- Confirm parent-focused language.

### Voice QA
- Confirm natural narration.
- Confirm proper pronunciation (dictionary verified).
- Confirm warm tone.
- Confirm child-appropriate pace.
- Confirm emotional direction included.

### Prompt QA
- Confirm correct prompt loaded.
- Confirm inputs accurate.
- Confirm outputs match requirements.
- Confirm no mixed prompts.

### Animation QA
- Confirm consistent style.
- Confirm soft, rounded shapes.
- Confirm warm colors.
- Confirm child-scale.
- Confirm no sharp edges.

### Packaging QA
- Confirm all files present.
- Confirm ZIP integrity.
- Confirm database updates.
- Confirm version consistency.
- Confirm brand consistency.

### Repository QA
- Confirm all cross-references accurate.
- Confirm no duplicated information.
- Confirm no missing files.
- Confirm consistent formatting.
- Confirm long-term maintainability.

## Quality Score System
Every episode receives a quality score (1-10) in `generation_log.json`. Episodes below 8.5 must be revised. Episodes below 7.0 must be regenerated.
