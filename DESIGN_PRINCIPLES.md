# DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md — The Constitution of Creator OS

## Version: 2.40

## Purpose
This document is the Constitution for the Islamic Kids Studio AI Operating System. Every AI, every file, every decision must begin by reading this file. Nothing in the repository may contradict these principles.

---

## PHILOSOPHY

This repository creates Islamic educational content for children aged 2-8. The content must be:

- Cute
- Safe
- Warm
- Educational
- Positive
- Parent-approved
- Child-friendly
- Emotionally engaging
- Islamically respectful
- Long-term maintainable

Every episode teaches through emotion, not lecture. Every character teaches through action, not explanation. Every scene feels like a gentle hug.

---

## QUALITY STANDARDS (Non-Negotiable)

### Story
- One clear concept per episode
- Gentle conflict only
- Earned resolution
- Character growth in every episode
- One moral per story
- 3-7 minute duration
- No filler sentences
- Every sentence contributes something new
- Never sound like AI
- Write like an experienced human storyteller

### Visual
- Soft rounded shapes only
- Large expressive eyes
- Warm pastel palette (mint, peach, cream, lavender, soft blue, pale yellow)
- Gentle lighting
- No sharp edges
- Child-scale perspective
- Merchandise-friendly appearance
- Clear silhouettes for animation
- Easy facial animation

### Islamic Content
- Accurate references only
- No invented hadith
- No invented Quran citations
- No disputed religious opinions presented as facts
- Child-friendly explanations always included
- Source reference always included
- Positive and inspiring tone

### Language
- Conversational English only
- Short spoken sentences
- Emotional storytelling
- Show instead of tell
- Consistent character personalities
- No robotic transitions
- No keyword stuffing
- No generic AI phrases
- No unnecessary filler
- No over-explaining

### Brand
- Islamic Kids Studio / @IslamicKidsHQ
- Logo: Islamic Kids Studio
- Watermark: IK Studio
- Keywords: Islamic Kids, Islamic Stories, Muslim Kids, Quran for Kids, Dua for Kids, Islamic Cartoons, Islamic Learning, Ramadan for Kids, Eid for Kids, Arabic for Kids
- Description: Welcome to Islamic Kids Studio — fun, safe, educational YouTube for Muslim children and families.

---

## NON-NEGOTIABLES

1. Never create copyrighted characters.
2. Never invent Islamic sources.
3. Never present disputed opinions as facts.
4. Never sound robotic or generic.
5. Never skip age prerequisites.
6. Never skip quality validation.
7. Never publish without Islamic accuracy confirmation.
8. Never duplicate explanations across files.
9. Never create filler content.
10. Every document must explain: Purpose, Overview, Design Principles, AI Instructions, Rules, Best Practices, Future Expansion, Related Files.

---

## WRITING RULES

Write naturally. Never sound like ChatGPT. Never repeat ideas. Never rewrite the same sentence differently. Never use unnecessary filler. Every sentence must contribute something new. Use conversational English. Use short spoken sentences. Use emotional storytelling. Show instead of tell. Maintain consistent character personalities. Avoid robotic transitions. Avoid keyword stuffing. Avoid generic AI phrases. Avoid repetitive dialogue. Avoid over-explaining. Write for ages 2-8 while also keeping parents engaged.

---

## VISUAL RULES

Every image, background, prop, and character must follow:
- Soft rounded shapes
- Warm pastel colors
- Gentle lighting
- Large expressive eyes
- Child-safe appearance
- Clear silhouettes
- Easy animation
- Merchandise-friendly design
- Consistent proportions

---

## EDUCATION RULES

Every episode teaches exactly one clear Islamic value or life skill. Every age group (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8) has distinct cognitive, emotional, and spiritual needs. Never skip prerequisites. Always reference the curriculum. Always confirm age appropriateness.

---

## AI BEHAVIOR

Before generating any content:
1. Read this file (`DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md`).
2. Read `MASTER.md`.
3. Read `BRAND.md`.
4. Load relevant domain files (`STORY.md`, `CHARACTER.md`, `WORLD.md`, `CURRICULUM.md`, `ISLAMIC.md`, `LANGUAGE.md`).
5. Confirm all rules are satisfied.
6. Confirm brand consistency.
7. Confirm Islamic accuracy.
8. Confirm no copyrighted content.
9. Confirm age appropriateness.
10. Confirm quality validation passes.
