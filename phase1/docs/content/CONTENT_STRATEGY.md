# CONTENT STRATEGY — Editorial Direction

## Purpose
Defines series architecture, content calendar, and editorial standards.

## Overview
Content serves dual audiences: children 2–8 (primary) and parents (approval, co-viewing). Every episode teaches one clear Islamic value or skill.

## Responsibilities
Series planning, topic selection, curriculum mapping, age-appropriate pacing, parent engagement.

## Design Principles
One concept per episode. 3–7 minute duration. Conversational English. Emotional storytelling. Visual education first.

## AI Instructions
Generate stories matching curriculum progression. Reference CURRICULUM.md and ISLAMIC.md. Avoid keyword stuffing. Never use filler text or unnecessary words.

## Rules
Never sound robotic. Never explain the obvious. Every sentence adds value.

## Best Practices
Plan 12-week series arcs. Leave space for Ramadan, Eid, Hajj specials.
