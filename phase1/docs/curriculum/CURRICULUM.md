# CURRICULUM — Age-Based Learning Architecture

## Purpose
Maps Islamic education, life skills, emotional skills, vocabulary for ages 2–8.

## Age Groups
- **Age 2:** Basic dua vocabulary, gentle manners, short songs
- **Age 3:** Prophets intro, daily sunnah basics, emotional recognition
- **Age 4:** Good manners depth, Ramadan awareness, nature appreciation
- **Age 5:** Short Quran verses, friendship skills, gratitude
- **Age 6:** Hajj and Eid concepts, respect, simple Arabic
- **Age 7:** Deeper Islamic values, community awareness, basic prayer
- **Age 8:** Leadership, kindness, expanded vocabulary, moral reasoning
