# STORY — Storytelling System

## Purpose
Defines how stories are structured, written, delivered.

## Overview
Every story teaches through emotion, not lecture. Gentle conflicts. Earned resolutions.

## Structure
1. Warm opening (15 sec)
2. Gentle conflict (2 min)
3. Character response (1 min)
4. Resolution with Islamic value (1 min)
5. Closing dua/song (30 sec)

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell.
- Character growth in every episode.
- One moral per story.
- Parent-engaging subtext.
- Timeless themes.

## AI Instructions
Write naturally. Never sound like ChatGPT. Avoid filler. Every sentence contributes. Conversational English. Short spoken sentences.

## Rules
- No robotic transitions
- No keyword stuffing
- No unnecessary adjectives
- No repetitive dialogue
- No over-explaining

## Best Practices
Plan 12-week series arcs. Leave space for seasonal specials (Ramadan, Eid, Hajj). Maintain consistent emotional tone across all episodes. Confirm every story references character profiles and world settings.

## Future Expansion
Story universe will expand with new character growth arcs, seasonal stories, multi-language adaptations, and parent co-viewing content.
