# Animation Directions (self-contained per shot)

## Block 1 — Scene 1
Zayd and Amira browsing a fruit stall, light and easy body language.
Duration: ~15s. Camera: static wide-medium. Emotion: joy/ease.

## Block 2 — Scene 2
Zayd's hand animates picking up the wallet, a beat of stillness, eyes
glancing side to side — tempted, uneasy, never panicked. Duration:
~45s. Camera: static medium. Emotion: worry (gentle, per
DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md's no-fear rule).

## Block 3 — Scene 3
Amira's concerned head-tilt, Zayd's shoulders dropping then steadying
as he talks it through and decides — visible shift from unsettled to
resolved. Duration: ~75s. Camera: slow push-in. Emotion: worry
resolving into resolve/calm.

## Block 4 — Scene 4
Zayd's posture is upright and clear as he explains; the stall owner's
surprised-then-warm reaction; a genuine smile exchange, no exaggerated
reward gesture. Duration: ~50s. Camera: slow pull-back. Emotion:
relief/gratitude.

## Block 5 — Scene 5
Walking animation, relaxed pace, Zayd's quiet dua delivered calmly, both
characters content. Duration: ~30s. Camera: static wide-medium.
Emotion: gratitude/contentment.
