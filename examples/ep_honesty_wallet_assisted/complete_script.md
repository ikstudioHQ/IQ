# Complete Script — The Day Zayd Chose to Be Honest

[SCENE 1 — env_market, midday]
AMIRA: These dates look perfect for Ummi's basket.
ZAYD: (spotting something) Amira, look—

[SCENE 2 — env_market]
ZAYD: (picking up a wallet) It's... someone's wallet. It has money in it.
ZAYD: (pausing, torn) No one's even looking...
(He holds it, unsettled, glancing around.)

[SCENE 3 — env_market, quieter corner]
AMIRA: Zayd? What's wrong?
ZAYD: I don't know what to do. Part of me wants to just... keep it.
AMIRA: It's not ours though.
ZAYD: (quietly) Sidq means telling the truth about it. And amanah means
taking care of it properly, since it isn't mine.
ZAYD: (steadier now) I've decided what's right. Now I'll trust Allah
with the rest.

[SCENE 4 — env_market, the fruit stall]
ZAYD: (to the stall owner) Excuse me — I found this wallet near your
stall. I think it might be yours, or someone dropped it here.
STALL OWNER: (checking, surprised) This is mine! I didn't even notice
it was gone. Thank you, truly — that was very honest of you.

[SCENE 5 — walking home]
ZAYD: (quietly, content) Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel.
AMIRA: (smiling) That felt like the right thing, didn't it?
ZAYD: It did.
