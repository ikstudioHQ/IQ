# Complete Script — Zayd's Lost Toy

[SCENE 1 — env_park, golden afternoon light]
ZAYD: Nuri, watch — my camel can jump THIS high today!
AMIRA: (laughing) That's the highest yet!
(Nuri bounces happily beside them. Warm, easy joy.)

[SCENE 2 — env_home, Zayd's room, evening]
ZAYD: (reaching for his shelf) Time for bed, little camel... 
ZAYD: (pausing) ...camel?
NARRATION: Zayd couldn't find his toy camel anywhere.
ZAYD: (searching under the bed) Not here...
ZAYD: (searching the toy basket) Not here either...
(Zayd sits back, shoulders low, worried.)

[SCENE 3 — env_home, Zayd's room]
UMMI LAYLA: (sitting beside him, gently) You've looked so hard already, habibi.
ZAYD: I looked everywhere, Ummi. What if it's really gone?
UMMI LAYLA: You did your best. Now — let's ask Allah to help us, and keep
looking together, calmly.
UMMI LAYLA: Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel.
ZAYD: (repeating, softer now) Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel.
(Zayd's shoulders relax. The searching continues, but gently now — not
frantic.)

[SCENE 4 — env_home, living room]
AMIRA: (thinking) Wait... did you bring him to the park in your bag?
ZAYD: (eyes widening) My park bag!
(Zayd runs to his bag, opens the side pocket — the toy camel is there.)
ZAYD: Alhamdulillah! You were right here the whole time!

[SCENE 5 — env_home, living room, family gathered]
ZAYD: (holding the toy camel close) Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel.
UMMI LAYLA: (smiling) Allah is always enough for us.
(Warm, quiet, close. Fade on the family together.)
