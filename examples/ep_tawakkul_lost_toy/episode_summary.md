# Episode Summary — Zayd's Lost Toy

**Episode ID:** ep_tawakkul_lost_toy
**Topic:** t_tawakkul (Trust in Allah)
**Series:** Islamic Kids Studio — Standalone / Season 2
**Target age:** 6-7
**Duration target:** ~210 seconds
**Characters:** Zayd (lead), Amira (supporting), Ummi Layla (supporting), Nuri (mascot)
**Environments:** env_park, env_home
**Concept:** concept_tawakkul
**Conflict:** cf_001 (Lost toy)
**Story pattern:** pattern_001 (Problem → Dua → Effort → Lesson)
**Curiosity hook:** hook_002
**Ending style:** end_001 (dua_close)

Zayd can't find his favorite wooden toy camel anywhere. After searching
worriedly, Ummi Layla gently reminds him that after trying his best, he
can trust Allah with the rest — they say a dua together, keep looking
calmly, and find the toy exactly where effort (not panic) led them.

This is the repository's first full end-to-end proof episode — the
exact topic that halted the pipeline before v2.5 (see CHANGELOG.md v2.5)
is now producible, and this package demonstrates the complete pipeline
actually running.
