# Animation Directions (self-contained per shot)

## Block 1 — Scene 1
Zayd mid-air jumping with toy camel raised, Amira's laugh animates with
a slight head-tilt-back, Nuri bounces on all fours with ears flopping.
Duration: ~15s. Camera: static wide-medium, one quick gentle whip to
follow the jump (only intentional fast movement in the episode — pure
joy beat). Lighting: warm golden park light, soft rim light on hair/wool.
Lip-sync: Zayd's line syncs on the jump apex. Emotion: joy, high energy
but controlled per DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md gentle-movement rule elsewhere.

## Block 2 — Scene 2
Zayd's search animates as a sequence: opening a toy basket lid, peering
under a bed frame, patting a shelf — each action slightly slower than
the last, shoulders progressively lowering. Duration: ~55s. Camera:
static medium shot throughout. Lighting: warm lamp, cooler ambient.
Lip-sync: minimal dialogue, mostly physical performance. Emotion: worry,
building gently — never sudden or startling.

## Block 3 — Scene 3
Ummi Layla sits, one hand resting gently near Zayd's shoulder (not
grabbing — reassuring). Zayd's breathing/shoulder animation visibly
slows and drops as the dua is said together, lips moving in sync on the
Arabic phrase. Duration: ~60s. Camera: slow push-in starting wide-close,
ending tight-close by the dua's end. Lighting: warm key light intensifies
slightly through the push-in. Emotion: hope, safety, calm.

## Block 4 — Scene 4
Amira's realization animates as a quick eyebrow-raise + head-tilt,
Zayd's run to the bag is energetic but not frantic, the reveal of the
toy camel triggers a full-body happy bounce. Duration: ~40s. Camera:
slow pull-back from medium-close to medium-wide. Lighting: brightens
warmly through the shot. Emotion: relief transitioning to joy.

## Block 5 — Scene 5
Family grouped closely, Zayd hugs the toy camel to his chest during the
dua line, all three characters' expressions soften into quiet smiles by
the final line. Duration: ~30s. Camera: static wide-medium, no movement
— stillness reinforces the calm closing per ending_styles.json's
dua_close style. Lighting: warm, even, soft, no harsh shadows. Emotion:
belonging, gratitude, warmth.
