# Complete Script — Zayd Waits (Test C, ~90 sec core scenes)

[SCENE 1 — env_home, window, late afternoon]
NARRATOR: Baba Ahmad promised a surprise on his way home. Zayd had been watching the door for what felt like forever.
ZAYD: (pacing) He said soon. It's BEEN soon.
AMIRA: Maybe soon means a different soon for grown-ups.

[SCENE 2 — Zayd, restless]
NARRATOR: Zayd wanted to run outside and check the street for the hundredth time. But he stopped.
ZAYD: (quieter) I already know he's coming. I just... don't know when.
NARRATOR: Zayd took a breath. "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel." Allah is enough for us, and He is the best One to take care of things.

[SCENE 3 — the door opens]
NARRATOR: And when Baba Ahmad finally walked in—
ZAYD: (relieved smile, not rushing) You came!
BABA AHMAD: I did. Thank you for waiting so patiently.
NARRATOR: Zayd had done what he could — he waited. The rest, he trusted.
