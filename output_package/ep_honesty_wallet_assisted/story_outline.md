# Story Outline (5-beat structure)

**Opening (15s):** Zayd and Amira at env_market, browsing a fruit stall.

**Conflict (2 min):** Zayd spots a wallet on the ground. He picks it up
— it has money in it. For a moment he's tempted to just keep it. He
feels torn and unsettled.

**Character Response (1 min):** Amira notices Zayd's hesitation. They
talk it through — not about a specific rule, but about what feels
right: sidq (telling the truth about finding it) and amanah (taking
care of it properly since it isn't theirs). Zayd decides, and says
qv_004's idea in his own words: "I've decided what's right — now I'll
trust Allah with the rest."

**Resolution (1 min):** Zayd brings the wallet to the stall owner and
explains he found it nearby. The owner is moved and thanks him warmly.
No reward is stated or implied beyond the owner's genuine gratitude.

**Closing (30s):** Zayd and Amira walk home. Zayd says the Hasbunallah
dua quietly, feeling calm and good about his choice.
