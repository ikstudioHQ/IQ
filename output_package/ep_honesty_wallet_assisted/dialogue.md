# Dialogue (full lines)
AMIRA: These dates look perfect for Ummi's basket.
ZAYD: Amira, look—
ZAYD: It's... someone's wallet. It has money in it.
ZAYD: No one's even looking...
AMIRA: Zayd? What's wrong?
ZAYD: I don't know what to do. Part of me wants to just... keep it.
AMIRA: It's not ours though.
ZAYD: Sidq means telling the truth about it. And amanah means taking care of it properly, since it isn't mine.
ZAYD: I've decided what's right. Now I'll trust Allah with the rest.
ZAYD: Excuse me — I found this wallet near your stall. I think it might be yours, or someone dropped it here.
STALL OWNER: This is mine! I didn't even notice it was gone. Thank you, truly — that was very honest of you.
ZAYD: Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel.
AMIRA: That felt like the right thing, didn't it?
ZAYD: It did.
