# Complete Script — Amira Chooses Her Words (Test B, ~90 sec core scenes)

[SCENE 1 — env_home, living room]
NARRATOR: Zayd and Amira were building a block tower — until it toppled.
ZAYD: (frustrated) You knocked it over!
AMIRA: (quickly, without thinking) You're so clumsy, you always ruin everything!

[SCENE 2 — close-up, Amira realizing]
NARRATOR: The words came out fast. Too fast. Amira saw Zayd's face fall, and something in her chest went quiet and sorry.
AMIRA: (softer) ...That wasn't a good thing to say.

[SCENE 3 — Amira and Zayd, resolving]
AMIRA: I'm sorry, Zayd. It was an accident, and I said something unkind instead of just telling you that.
ZAYD: (small smile) It's okay.
NARRATOR: When you're not sure your words are kind — it's better to pause, or say nothing at all, than to let unkind words fly.
