# Story Outline (5-beat structure, STORY.md)

**Opening (15s):** Zayd and Amira play happily at env_park with Zayd's
small wooden toy camel and Nuri bounding around them. Warm, joyful.

**Conflict (2 min):** Back at env_home, Zayd reaches for his toy camel
before bedtime and it's gone. [Hook: "Zayd couldn't find his toy camel
anywhere..."] He searches his room with growing worry — under the bed,
in the toy basket, in his school bag. No luck. His shoulders slump.

**Character Response (1 min):** Ummi Layla notices and sits with him.
She doesn't just say "don't worry" — she reminds him: "You've looked so
hard already. Now let's ask Allah to help, and keep looking calmly
together." They say dua_005 together. Zayd's worry visibly softens
(effort continues, this time steady rather than frantic — pattern_001's
"Effort" beat).

**Resolution (1 min):** Amira remembers Zayd carried the toy camel in
his park bag. They check — it's there, tucked in a side pocket. Zayd
lights up: "Alhamdulillah!" Relief and quiet joy.

**Closing (30s):** Family gathers. Zayd holds his toy camel and says the
Hasbunallah dua once more, this time not from worry but from thankfulness.
Ends on end_001 (dua_close) — calm, warm, no rush.
