# Description

Zayd can't find his favorite toy camel anywhere! With Ummi Layla's gentle
guidance, he learns that after trying his very best, he can trust Allah
with the rest — through the beautiful dua "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal
wakeel." A warm, gentle story about tawakkul (trust in Allah) for
children ages 5-8.

New episodes every week from Islamic Kids Studio — join Zayd, Amira, and
Nuri as they learn about Islam through gentle, original stories.
