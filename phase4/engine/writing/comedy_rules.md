# COMEDY RULES — Gentle Comedy for Children

## Purpose
Defines how gentle humor is used in Islamic Kids Studio episodes.

## Rules
- Comedy must be gentle, safe, and kind.
- Never laugh at someone's pain.
- Use silly situations, not sarcastic ones.
- Use playful language, not mocking language.
- Keep humor respectful of Islamic values.
- Make both children and parents smile.
