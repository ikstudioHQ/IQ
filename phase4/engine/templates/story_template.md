# STORY TEMPLATE — Episode Story Structure

## Template Sections
1. **Title**: Warm, engaging, includes value or character name.
2. **Age Group**: Confirm from curriculum.
3. **Main Character**: Confirm active character.
4. **Setting**: Confirm environment from world database.
5. **Opening Scene**: 15 seconds. Warm, gentle action.
6. **Conflict Introduction**: 2 minutes. Small, age-appropriate problem.
7. **Character Response**: 1 minute. Kind, brave, or gentle action.
8. **Islamic Value Resolution**: 1 minute. Value shown through action.
9. **Closing**: 30 seconds. Warm dua or short song.
10. **Total Duration**: 3-7 minutes.

## Usage
Fill in each section using `STORY.md`, `CHARACTER.md`, `WORLD.md`, `CURRICULUM.md`, and `ISLAMIC.md`. Confirm all references are accurate before generating.
