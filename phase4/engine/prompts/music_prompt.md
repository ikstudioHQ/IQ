# MUSIC PROMPT — Music & Sound Design Module

## Purpose
Defines music and sound design requirements for episodes.

## Rules
- Gentle, warm, child-friendly music.
- Soft instruments: piano, acoustic guitar, soft strings, gentle percussion.
- No harsh sounds, no loud drums, no scary tones.
- Music supports the emotional story, not dominates it.
- Include short nasheed-style closing songs where appropriate.
- Confirm brand warmth in every sound choice.
