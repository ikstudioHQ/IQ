# REVIEW CHECKLIST — Final Review Process

## Steps
1. **AI Quality Check**: Load `qa_checklist.md`. Confirm all PASS.
2. **Brand Check**: Confirm `BRAND.md` compliance.
3. **Islamic Check**: Confirm `ISLAMIC.md` compliance. Confirm no invented sources.
4. **Parent Review** (new series only): Confirm parent-approval.
5. **Packaging Check**: Confirm all files present. Confirm ZIP integrity.
6. **Database Update**: Confirm `current_state.json`, `last_episode.json`, and `generation_log.json` updated.

## Rules
- Never skip review steps.
- Always document errors.
- Never publish without Islamic accuracy confirmation.
- Always confirm brand consistency.
