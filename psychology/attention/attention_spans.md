# Attention Span Research — Age 2-8

## Version: 1.1

## Key Finding
Children aged 2-8 have effective attention spans of 3-7 minutes for educational media content.
- Age 2-3: 2-4 minutes
- Age 4-5: 4-6 minutes
- Age 6-8: 5-7 minutes
