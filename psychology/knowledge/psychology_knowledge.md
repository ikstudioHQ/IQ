# Psychology Knowledge — Parent & Child Psychology

## Version: 1.1

## Key Insights (Verified)

### Parent Psychology
- Parents seek content that is safe, educational, positive, and Islamically respectful.
- Parents co-view with children aged 2-5.
- Parents approve content that teaches values through gentle stories, not lectures.
- Parents value merchandise-friendly characters that they feel comfortable buying for their children.
- Parents respond positively to short, warm descriptions that explain the educational value clearly.

### Child Psychology (Ages 2-8)
- Children aged 2-4 learn through simple repetition, emotional connection, and visual storytelling.
- Children aged 5-6 begin understanding deeper moral concepts through character actions.
- Children aged 7-8 can handle more complex emotional and moral reasoning.
- Children learn best when characters express emotions clearly and naturally.
- Children retain lessons when stories include a warm closing with a dua or song.

### Attention Psychology
- Maximum effective attention span for ages 2-8: 3-7 minutes per episode.
- Attention increases when content includes gentle conflict followed by calm resolution.
- Attention decreases with filler, repetition, robotic language, or over-explaining.
- Emotional warmth increases retention.

### Motivation Psychology
- Children are motivated by kindness, warmth, and positive emotional feedback.
- Children respond to stories where characters grow a little in every episode.
- Parents are motivated by content that teaches Islamic values and life skills clearly.
- Both audiences are motivated by cute, safe, warm, and positive content.
